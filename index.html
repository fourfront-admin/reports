⬆ Double Down On
• Value/calculation statics: "Berekening" and "Code" are the most efficient formats in the account by a wide margin. Produce 3–4 new angles (cost per hour of fun, cost vs. a night out, cost vs. streaming subscriptions).

• Kill the assumption that video-first equals performance. Static outsells video on every single metric in this account. Reallocate 100% of conversion budget to static.
• Stop running multiple low-budget copies of the same concept ("Waar vo #1" has 3 versions with mediocre CPPs). Consolidate spend behind the winner or kill the angle.
