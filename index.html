30 ads · €2802 spend · 125 purchases
✅ What's Working
• FIRST NEEMS videos are the engine — three variants all crushing it with ROAS 7-11x and consistent purchases. The Weeztix-direct link versions outperform website variants on volume.
• TIKKET UPDEET #2 is the stealth winner — both the original (17.3x ROAS, €10.57 CPP) and copy (12.97x ROAS, €11.49 CPP) are your cheapest ticket sales in the entire account. Urgency-style countdowns clearly convert.
• One AFTERMOEVIE _ WT variant hit 52.46x ROAS at €6.26 CPP — the best single ad in the account. Aftermovie nostalgia + Weeztix link is a proven formula when it catches the right audience.
❌ What's Not Working
• Multi Creative _ Event spent €96 with zero purchases — carousel/multi-format is not your thing. This audience wants single-video hits, not browseable creative.
• McDrummer location-specific ads (Aalst, Stronteduur) are spend sinks — €95 combined, zero purchases, despite absurdly inflated CTRs (920%+). High engagement, no intent. People watch but don't buy.
• Website-destination variants consistently underperform Weeztix-direct — McDrummer Sold _ Site (0.73x ROAS, €94.78 CPP) vs McDrummer Sold _ WT (6.03x ROAS, €16.54 CPP). The extra click kills conversion.
⬆ Double Down On
• Produce more TIKKET UPDEET-style urgency countdowns — they convert at the lowest CPP in the account. Make versions for every ticket milestone (50% sold, last 100 tickets, final wave).
• Keep the FIRST NEEMS / lineup-reveal format as your awareness-to-purchase workhorse. Create new variants per artist or lineup wave — the format clearly resonates.
• Always link to Weeztix, never to website. Every Weeztix variant outperforms its website twin. Remove the friction.
🛑 Kill or Rethink
• Kill all website-destination variants immediately — McDrummer Sold _ Site, FIRST NEEMS Website, TIKKET UPDEET _ Site. They bleed money compared to Weeztix-direct twins.
• Kill Multi Creative _ Event. Zero purchases. This format doesn't work for your audience.
• Kill McDrummer Aalst and McDrummer Stronteduur — vanity CTR, zero revenue. Location-specific teasers without a clear ticket hook are entertainment, not advertising.
