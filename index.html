✅ What's Working
• Video Functional Drinks format crushes statics on engagement — CTRs 291–415% vs 102–146% for statics. The format clearly resonates.
• Overall engagement rates are strong across the board, suggesting creative quality is not the core problem — measurement is.
• Consumer-facing angles (USPs, Routine, Voorjaarsboost) show healthy engagement signals even on minimal budget.
❌ What's Not Working
• Zero purchases across ALL 10 ads — because there is no webshop. You are measuring fish by their ability to climb trees. The Meta pixel has no purchase event to fire, so the algorithm has zero conversion data to learn from. This is a structural failure, not a creative one.
• Three B2B ads (Açai Koffiebar, This is how we bowl, Upgrade Brunch) burned €1,199 — 67% of total spend — prospecting coffee bars and restaurants on a consumer social platform. LinkedIn or direct outreach is where B2B belongs.
• Four promising video ads (Functional Drinks 1–3, Routine) got a combined €27 in spend. Your best-engaging format is being starved while your worst-fit ads eat the budget.
⬆ Double Down On
• Video-first creative in the Functional Drinks mold — the engagement gap over statics is 2–3x. Produce more short-form video (Reels/Stories native) around product use occasions.
• Retail-aware CTAs and creative — every ad should answer 'where do I buy this?' Push people to Bio-Planet/Delhaize with store locator links or in-store imagery. This is your only conversion path.
• Belgian endorser content — Bekkari, Binge, Gruyaert are unused firepower on Meta where influencer/authority creative dominates. Build video and static series around them.
🛑 Kill or Rethink
• Kill all B2B ads on Meta immediately. €1,199 spent reaching restaurant owners on Instagram is money on fire. Move B2B to LinkedIn, email, or direct sales.
• Kill purchase-optimized campaign objectives. Switch to Reach, Video Views, or Traffic (landing page views). Without a purchase event, the algorithm is flying blind and you're paying for it.
• Kill the static-heavy creative mix. Statics are underperforming video by 2–3x on engagement. Reserve statics for retargeting only.
