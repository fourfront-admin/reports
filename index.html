✅ What's Working
• FIRST NEEMS videos are the engine: 3 Weeztix variants combined for 53 purchases at ROAS 7-11x with CPP €17-24 — consistent volume and efficiency at the highest spend in the account.
• TIKKET UPDEET #2 variants are efficiency kings: ROAS 12.97-17.3x at CPP €10-11, cheapest conversions in the entire account — scarcity/urgency messaging hits hardest.
• Weeztix destination wins every head-to-head: McDrummer Sold WT (ROAS 6.03x) vs Site (0.73x), TIKKET UPDEET WT variants crush Site variants. The pattern is undeniable and consistent across every creative concept.
❌ What's Not Working
• McDrummer location variants (Aalst at 925% CTR, Stronteduur at 920% CTR) are pure engagement bait: €95 combined spend, zero purchases — people watch the spectacle but never click through to buy.
• Multi Creative _ Event is dead on arrival: €96 burned, 0 purchases — this audience converts on single video, not carousel or multi-asset formats.
• Website/Site destinations consistently bleed money vs Weeztix equivalents — McDrummer Sold Site lost money at ROAS 0.73x while the identical creative via Weeztix returned 6.03x.
⬆ Double Down On
• Produce more TIKKET UPDEET-style urgency videos — specifically the #2 format which converts at €10-11 CPP. This is your cheapest conversion path by a wide margin.
• Keep feeding the FIRST NEEMS lineup reveal machine with new artist announcements — it's your highest-volume format and scales reliably without ROAS degradation across variants.
• Aftermovie format has explosive upside: one variant hit 52.46x ROAS at €6.26 CPP. Produce multiple cuts with different opening hooks to isolate what made that variant a unicorn.
🛑 Kill or Rethink
• Pause all McDrummer location-specific variants immediately — Aalst and Stronteduur are burning cash on vanity views with zero revenue to show for it.
• Kill every Website/Site destination ad where a Weeztix equivalent exists or can be created — you are literally paying more for worse results on every single one.
• Retire Multi Creative _ Event format entirely and do not revisit — this Facebook audience converts on single video creative, period.
