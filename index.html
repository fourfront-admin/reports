✅ What's Working
• Engagement is massive — "Static _ Artikel _ 01" at 457% CTR and "Static _ Thuis vs Publiek _ 02" at 237% CTR show people are clicking and interacting with the content, so the creative hooks land.
• The comparison angle (Thuis vs Publiek) earned the most algorithmic spend (€455 across 3 variants), meaning Meta's system found signal in that audience — people weighing home vs. public charging are genuinely in-market.
• Article-style creative drives the highest engagement per euro, confirming Belgian EV buyers respond to educational, editorial content over hard-sell product ads.
❌ What's Not Working
• €1,045 spent, zero purchases across ALL 7 ads — this is not a creative problem, this is a broken funnel. Either conversion tracking is misconfigured, the landing page doesn't convert, or you're optimizing for purchases on a high-consideration product that requires a lead-gen approach (quote request, consultation booking).
• Every single ad is a static image — for a premium installation product that costs thousands of euros, there is zero social proof, zero video walkthrough, zero testimonial content to build the trust required for this price point.
• All three creative themes (Artikel, Thuisladen, Thuis vs Publiek) are top-of-funnel awareness angles with no mid-funnel or bottom-funnel creative to catch warmed-up prospects — the funnel has a ceiling but no floor.
⬆ Double Down On
• Thuis vs Publiek comparison angle — this is the strongest strategic hook for Belgium where public charging infrastructure is patchy; produce video and carousel versions showing cost-per-km savings over 1, 3, and 5 years.
• Article/editorial style content — the engagement proves Belgian audiences trust informative content; convert this into lead magnets ("Gratis gids: thuisladen in België") that capture emails instead of asking for a purchase.
• Localized Belgian proof points — none of these ads reference Belgian subsidies, installation regulations, or local customer stories; adding "Vlaamse premie" or real Belgian installation photos would dramatically increase relevance and trust.
🛑 Kill or Rethink
• Stop optimizing for purchases — EV charging infrastructure is a €1,000-5,000+ considered purchase; nobody buys a laadpaal from a cold Meta ad. Switch every campaign to lead generation (quote requests, callback bookings, guide downloads) immediately.
• Kill all three "variant 01/02/03" splits until the funnel is fixed — you're burning €1,045 A/B testing creative variations when the real problem is post-click; no amount of new static ads will fix a broken landing page or wrong campaign objective.
• Stop running only static images — this product category demands trust-building formats (video installations, customer testimonials, interactive calculators) that statics cannot deliver.
